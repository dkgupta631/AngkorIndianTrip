The Plan

Three days, in order

Every stop is arranged so you see Angkor at its best light — mornings at the great temples, evenings for rest.

01ARRIVAL

Arrival & accommodation

Land in Siem Reap and settle into your hotel. The evening opens with the sights beyond the temples — a floating village, a jungle waterfall, and dinner with a live Apsara dance.

  • Floating Village
  • Kulen Mountain Waterfall
  • Dinner & Apsara Dance Show
02ANGKOR DAY

The main Angkor circuit

A full day through the temples Angkor is known for, before returning to the hotel for the night.

  • Angkor Wat Temple
  • Banteay Kdei Temple
  • Angkor Thom City
  • Ta Keo Temple
  • Ta Prohm Temple (Tomb Raider)
03BIG CIRCLE

The Big Circle & Banteay Srei

A wider loop out to Banteay Srei — the “Pink Temple,” 40km out — and the lesser-visited temples along the way.

  • Banteay Srei (Pink Temple)
  • East Mebon Temple
  • Preah Khan Temple
  • Ta Som Temple
  • Pre Rup Temple
  • Neak Pean Temple
Go Travel. Discover.

Cambodia, through Angkor Wat

Cambodia’s Angkor region packs a thousand years of Khmer history into a few square kilometres — temple-mountains, moated cities, and jungle ruins, all built between the 9th and 13th centuries. Angkor Wat itself began as a Hindu temple dedicated to Vishnu, a detail that still surprises most Indian travellers who visit.

This package sticks to Siem Reap and the Angkor circuit — no rushed multi-city hop — so three days is enough to see it properly.
