Angkor Wat is a magnificent symbol of Cambodia’s rich heritage and architectural brilliance — and, through Lord Vishnu, carries a forgotten cultural bond with India. This 3-day Siem Reap tour covers the main Angkor circuit and the Big Circle, with air ticket, visa and an Indian-speaking escort included, so you can focus on the temples rather than the paperwork.

Day 1 — Arrival

Arrival & accommodation

Land in Siem Reap and check into your hotel. In the evening, visit a floating village and the Kulen Mountain waterfall, then close the day with dinner and a live Apsara dance show.

Day 2 — Angkor Day

The main Angkor circuit

A full day covering Angkor Wat Temple, Banteay Kdei, Angkor Thom City, Ta Keo Temple, and Ta Prohm (the "Tomb Raider" temple), before returning to the hotel for the night.

Day 3 — Big Circle

Big Circle & Banteay Srei

A wider loop out to Banteay Srei, the pink sandstone temple 40km away, along with East Mebon, Preah Khan, Ta Som, Pre Rup and Neak Pean. Transfer to the airport for your flight home.
