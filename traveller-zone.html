Before You Fly

Documents & bookings to sort first

Passport

Valid for at least 6 months from your date of arrival in Cambodia, with two blank pages for the visa stamp.

Cambodia Visa

Indian passport holders need a visa — most travellers apply for the e-Visa online before departure.

  • e-Visa: ~USD 30, approved in about 3 business days
  • Visa on arrival is also available at Siem Reap airport
  • Standard stay: up to 30 days

Flights & Hotel

Both are booked as part of your AngkorIndianTrip package — confirmed tickets and hotel vouchers are shared before you fly.

Travel Insurance

Not included in the package — we'd recommend picking up a short-trip policy before departure. Ask us and we can point you to an option.

Passport Photos & Copies

Carry 2 passport-size photos and photocopies of your passport's first two pages, separate from the original.

Save Our Number

Your escort's contact and our 24/7 support line are shared before departure — save them to your phone before you fly.

Good To Know

Cambodia at a glance

Currency

The Cambodian Riel (KHR) is the official currency, but US Dollars are accepted almost everywhere in Siem Reap — carry small USD notes for temple entries and tips.

Best Time to Visit

November to March is the dry, cooler season and the most comfortable time to explore the temples. April–May is hot; June–October is the rainy season.

What to Pack

Light, breathable clothing, a hat and sunscreen, comfortable walking shoes, and shoulders/knees covered for temple visits.

Time Zone & Language

Cambodia is on ICT (UTC+7), an hour and a half ahead of India. Khmer is the official language; English is widely understood around Siem Reap's tourist areas.

Electricity

230V, mostly Type A/C/G sockets. Carry a universal adapter — Indian 3-pin plugs don't always fit.

SIM & Connectivity

Local prepaid SIMs are available at Siem Reap airport, or activate an international roaming pack before you leave India.
